Dining at Hotel Nainarr

A culinary journey through authentic flavours and premium cuisine

Our Restaurant

Where every meal is a celebration of taste and tradition

The Nainarr Kitchen

Our in-house restaurant is the heart of Hotel Nainarr's dining experience. Serving a rich spread of authentic South Indian cuisine alongside carefully curated North Indian and continental dishes, we cater to every palate.

From the crispy morning dosas and filter coffee to hearty lunch thalis and elegant dinner spreads, our kitchen team brings passion and expertise to every dish. We use fresh, locally sourced ingredients to ensure the highest quality in every meal.

Whether you are dining in our restaurant or ordering room service, the Hotel Nainarr dining experience is designed to be warm, satisfying, and memorable.

What We Serve

A diverse menu for every taste and occasion

South Indian Cuisine

Relish the authentic flavours of Tamil Nadu with our traditional South Indian spread — idli, dosa, sambar, rasam, rice meals, and more. Every dish is prepared with traditional recipes and fresh ingredients.

North Indian Cuisine

From rich curries and tandoori specialties to fragrant biryanis and fresh rotis, our North Indian menu brings the best of the subcontinent's culinary heritage to your table.

Continental & Snacks

For guests who prefer lighter fare, we offer a selection of continental dishes, sandwiches, soups, and snacks — perfect for a quick bite or a relaxed meal any time of day.

Beverages & Refreshments

Start your morning with our signature filter coffee or a refreshing fresh juice. We also serve a variety of hot and cold beverages, including teas, soft drinks, and mocktails.
